# Mediterranean Grilled Skewers with Sheet Pan Chickpea Rice

A vibrant, grill-friendly spring meal perfect for a backyard gathering. Features lemon herb grilled chicken, marinated tofu and veggie skewers, a chickpea rice pilaf baked in a foil pan, and a light cucumber tomato salad. Everything is gluten-free and dairy-free except the optional pita.

## Lemon Herb Chicken Thigh Skewers

**Ingredients:**
- 1.5–2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- Juice + zest of 2 lemons
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary or 2 tsp dried
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh thyme or 2 tsp dried
- Optional: pinch of chili flakes
- Optional: fresh parsley, chives, or basil (chopped)

**Instructions:**
1. Mix all marinade ingredients in a bowl or bag.
2. Add chicken and marinate for at least 1 hour or overnight.
3. Skewer and grill over medium heat for 6–8 minutes per side, until fully cooked and browned.

## Marinated Veggie + Tofu Skewers (or Grill Basket)

**Ingredients:**
- 1 block firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- Mushrooms, zucchini, bell peppers, onions, cherry tomatoes
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano or thyme
- Salt to taste

**Instructions:**
1. Toss tofu in a **separate small marinade** of lemon, olive oil, garlic, salt, and herbs.
2. Marinate veggies with similar seasoning in another bowl (especially if mushrooms are included).
3. Skewer or grill in a basket for ~5–7 minutes, turning occasionally until lightly charred and tender.

## Sheet Pan Chickpea Rice Pilaf

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups basmati rice (rinsed)
- 1 can diced tomatoes (with juice)
- 1 can chickpeas (drained)
- 2.5 cups water
- 1/2 cup peas (frozen is fine)
- 1/2 cup carrots, shredded or diced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- Optional: turmeric, herbs, or lemon slices for added flavor

**Instructions:**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. In a large oiled sheet pan or foil baking dish, combine all ingredients.
3. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 45–50 minutes.
4. Fluff with a fork before serving. Top with grilled veggies or fresh herbs if desired.

## Quick Tahini Dressing (Squeeze Bottle Friendly)

**Ingredients:**
- 3 tbsp tahini
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- Salt to taste
- 2–4 tbsp cold water, to thin

**Instructions:**
1. Stir everything together until creamy and pourable.
2. Add more water as needed.
3. Store in a squeeze bottle and serve with chicken, tofu, or rice.

## Simple Cucumber Tomato Salad

**Ingredients:**
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 large cucumber, chopped
- Salt & pepper to taste
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Optional: chopped parsley, basil, or chives

**Instructions:**
1. Toss all ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

## Optional Add-ons:
- Pita bread (for those who can eat gluten)
- Hummus or olives
- Fresh herbs for garnish

## Notes:

This recipe came out of a planning session for a spring "Friends Day" gathering with grilled foods and easy-to-share dishes. The user had chicken thighs marinating and wanted to keep everything **simple, grilled, and family-friendly**. 

They planned to:
- Use **a large foil sheet pan** for the rice and chickpeas
- Grill **veggies ahead of time** and throw them on top of the rice
- Use garden herbs (basil, parsley, chives) in the marinades
- Keep the sauces minimal, using **a squeeze bottle tahini dressing** instead of a dairy-based sauce
- Include **tofu** in a light marinade for vegetarian guests

This meal is designed to be **low stress, flavorful, and easily portable**, ideal for a spring picnic-style cookout.
