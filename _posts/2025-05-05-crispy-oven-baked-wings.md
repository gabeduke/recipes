# Crispy Oven-Baked Chicken Wings

These oven-baked chicken wings turn out crispy, juicy, and perfect for tossing in your favorite sauce. No deep-frying needed!

## Ingredients
- 2–3 lbs chicken wings, separated at joints, tips removed
- 1 tbsp baking powder (aluminum-free preferred)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Optional: your favorite sauce (Buffalo, BBQ, honey garlic, etc.)

## Instructions
1. **Preheat oven** to 425°F (220°C). Line a baking sheet with foil or parchment and place a wire rack on top.
2. **Dry the wings** thoroughly with paper towels. Moisture is the enemy of crispy skin.
3. In a large bowl, **toss the wings** with baking powder, salt, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and black pepper until evenly coated.
4. Arrange the wings **in a single layer** on the wire rack, making sure they are not touching.
5. **Bake for 40–50 minutes**, flipping halfway through, until golden and crispy. Use an instant-read thermometer to check that internal temp reaches 165°F.
6. If using sauce, **toss wings** with your desired sauce after baking.
7. For sauced wings, return to the oven for **5–7 more minutes** to caramelize. Optionally broil for 2–3 minutes, watching closely to avoid burning.

## Notes
- **Baking powder** (not baking soda) is essential here; it raises pH and helps break down proteins in the skin, promoting browning and crispiness.
- **Drying the wings** before seasoning is one of the most important steps for a crispy result.
- **Wire rack method** allows air to circulate and fat to render away, mimicking a fried texture.
- **High oven temperature** (425°F or higher) is crucial for rendering fat and achieving crisp skin.
- You can **marinate** the wings beforehand for extra flavor but be sure to dry them thoroughly before baking.
- From our conversation: these tips help achieve crisp wings without frying, and tossing them in sauce after baking (plus a short return to the oven) gives you that sticky, flavorful finish you’re looking for.

## Optional Sauces
- **Buffalo Sauce**: ¼ cup hot sauce + 2 tbsp melted butter
- **Honey Garlic**: 3 tbsp honey + 2 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp minced garlic
- **BBQ Glaze**: Your favorite BBQ sauce thinned with a splash of vinegar or water
