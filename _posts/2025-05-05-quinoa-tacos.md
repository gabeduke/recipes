# Quinoa Tacos

These vegetarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free tacos are built on a flavorful quinoa base and loaded with fresh toppings and creamy sauces. Great for weeknights or taco night with a twist!

## Ingredients

### Quinoa Base
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 2 cups vegetable broth (or water with 1 tsp bouillon)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- Salt to taste
- Optional: 1/2 cup cooked black beans or lentils
- Optional: 1/4 cup chopped toasted walnuts or sunflower seeds

### Quick Pickled Topping
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp sugar

### Fresh Toppings
- Shredded cabbage or lettuce
- Diced tomato
- Diced avocado or mango
- Chopped cilantro
- Sliced scallions
- Your favorite hot sauce or salsa

### Optional Sauces
**Lime-Cilantro Tahini**
- 1/4 cup tahini
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 2–4 tbsp water to thin
- Salt to taste
- Handful of chopped cilantro

**Avocado Crema**
- 1 ripe avocado
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/4 cup unsweetened dairy-free yogurt
- 1 clove garlic
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. **Cook the Quinoa Base**
   - In a saucepan, combine quinoa, broth, and spices. Bring to a boil.
   - Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes or until water is absorbed.
   - Fluff with a fork and stir in cooked beans and/or toasted nuts if using.

2. **Prepare the Quick Pickled Onion**
   - In a small bowl, combine red onion with lime juice, salt, and sugar.
   - Let sit while quinoa cooks (at least 10–15 minutes).

3. **Make Sauce (Optional)**
   - For either sauce, combine ingredients in a blender or bowl and mix until smooth.

4. **Assemble the Tacos**
   - Warm your favorite gluten-free tortillas.
   - Add a scoop of quinoa mixture, followed by pickled onions, fresh toppings, and sauce.

## Notes

- Cooking quinoa in broth and adding spices like cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika adds much more flavor than plain water.
- Toasted nuts/seeds give the quinoa a great texture and richness.
- Quick pickled veggies are an easy way to add brightness and acidity to balance the earthy quinoa.
- The tahini and avocado sauces are dairy-free, creamy options that elevate the tacos.
- These tacos are very flexible—swap toppings based on what’s fresh or in the fridge.
