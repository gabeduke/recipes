# Banana Bread Base Mix (Pantry & Gift Version)

## Pantry/Gift Jar Dry Mix:
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour  
- 1 teaspoon baking soda  
- 1/2 teaspoon salt  
- 3/4 cup sugar (white or brown)  
- *(Optional: 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon)*

---

## Add These When Ready to Bake:
- 1/2 cup melted butter  
- 2 large eggs  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract  
- 1 to 1 1/2 cups mashed or shredded ingredient (see table below)

**Instructions**:
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.  
2. Mix wet ingredients with dry mix until just combined.  
3. Pour into a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan.  
4. Bake for **50–60 minutes**, until a toothpick comes out clean.  
5. Cool in pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.

---

## Swap Banana for Other Mix-Ins:

| Bread Type     | Wet Ingredient                                      |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Banana Bread   | 3 mashed bananas                                     |
| Zucchini Bread | 1 1/2 cups shredded zucchini (squeezed dry)          |
| Pumpkin Bread  | 1 cup pumpkin purée                                  |
| Carrot Bread   | 1 1/2 cups finely shredded carrots                   |
| Apple Bread    | 1 1/2 cups grated apple (drain excess juice)         |
