# Cilantro-Lime Tahini Sauce with Jalapeño

A bold and creamy tahini sauce perfect for drizzling over grilled cauliflower and chickpeas. This sauce brings together the richness of tahini with the brightness of lime, the freshness of cilantro, and a subtle kick from jalapeño.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup tahini  
- 1/4 cup water (adjust for consistency)  
- Juice of 1 lime  
- 1 clove garlic  
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro  
- 1 small jalapeño (seeded for mild, leave seeds for heat)  
- 1 tbsp olive oil (optional, for smoother texture)  
- Salt to taste  
- Optional: 1 tsp maple syrup or honey (to balance bitterness)

## Instructions

1. Add tahini, water, lime juice, garlic, cilantro, and jalapeño to a blender or food processor.
2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
3. Taste and adjust:  
   - Add water to thin, if needed.  
   - Add salt or sweetener to balance flavors.  
4. Use immediately or refrigerate for up to 5 days.

## Notes

This sauce was created as part of a grilled meal idea using a grill basket. The original dish featured cauliflower florets and chickpeas, tossed in olive oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, cumin, salt, and pepper, then grilled for 15–20 minutes while stirring occasionally.

This tahini sauce complements the smoky grilled vegetables perfectly, especially when served in bowls or wraps, or drizzled over rice or quinoa. The jalapeño adds a touch of heat that balances the creamy and citrusy base, while cilantro and lime keep it fresh and vibrant.

You can thicken the sauce for dipping by reducing the water, or use it as a dressing by thinning it further with extra lime juice or water.
