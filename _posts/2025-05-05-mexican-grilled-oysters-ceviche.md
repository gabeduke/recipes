# Mexican-Style Grilled Oysters & Shrimp Ceviche

Recipes for a surprise 70th birthday party: grilled Virginia oysters with two Mexican-inspired toppings, and a shrimp or fish ceviche. Designed for easy prep and sharing at a family gathering.

## Ingredient List

### Fresh Produce
- 1 head garlic
- 8–10 limes
- 2–3 jalapeños
- 1–2 serranos or habaneros (optional)
- 2 red onions
- 1 bunch cilantro
- 2 medium tomatoes
- 2 ears fresh corn (or 1.5 cups frozen)
- 1 small cucumber (optional, for ceviche)
- 1 bunch green onions (optional garnish)
- Avocados (for guac and ceviche topping)

### Pantry / Dry Goods
- Olive oil
- Butter or dairy-free butter
- Mayonnaise or DF mayo
- Chipotle, guajillo, or Tajín chili powder
- Salsa macha (store-bought or homemade)
- Salt and black pepper
- Sugar (for ceviche balance)

### Dairy / Alt
- Cotija or vegan parmesan (or omit)

### Protein
- 1 dozen+ Virginia oysters
- 1/2–1 lb fish (e.g., snapper, halibut, or flounder) or deveined frozen shrimp

---

## Grilled Oysters with Chile-Lime Butter

**Make-Ahead Prep:**
- Mix the following into a compound butter:
  - 1/2 cup softened butter
  - 2 cloves minced garlic
  - Juice of 2 limes
  - 1 tsp chili powder
  - Pinch of salt
- Form into a log using parchment or plastic wrap and chill or freeze.

**Grill Instructions:**
1. Shuck oysters and place on grill, shell-side down.
2. Add a pat of the chile-lime butter to each.
3. Grill for 4–6 minutes until butter is melted and oysters are bubbling.
4. Optional: garnish with chopped green onion or cilantro.

---

## Grilled Oysters Elote-Style

**Make-Ahead Prep:**
- Mix the following:
  - 1/3 cup mayo
  - 1/2 tsp chili powder
  - Juice of 1 lime
  - Pinch of salt
- Crumble cotija or prep vegan cheese alternative.

**Grill Instructions:**
1. Grill shucked oysters until they begin to firm up (~4 minutes).
2. Add 1 tsp of the mayo mixture to each.
3. Sprinkle with cotija or vegan parm.
4. Optional: torch or broil briefly for caramelization.
5. Garnish with chopped cilantro.

---

## Shrimp or Fish Ceviche

**Ingredients:**
- 1/2 lb thawed shrimp or diced white fish
- Juice of 4 limes
- 1/4 cup diced red onion
- 1/2 diced jalapeño
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 diced tomato
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- Optional: 1/4 cup diced cucumber, 1/2 diced avocado, splash of olive oil

**Instructions:**
1. Dice shrimp or fish into 1/2" cubes.
2. Marinate in lime juice, red onion, jalapeño, and salt for 4–8 hours in the fridge.
3. Before serving, drain excess liquid.
4. Stir in tomato, cilantro, optional cucumber, and avocado.
5. Serve chilled with chips or tostadas.

## Notes

- These recipes are for a surprise 70th birthday party where family is bringing tacos and guacamole.
- Virginia oysters will be grilled — preferred over raw — and we’re trying Mexican-style toppings instead of the usual French ones.
- Recipes are designed to share ingredients and minimize last-minute prep.
- Shrimp was already in the freezer and is being used instead of raw oysters in ceviche.
- Ceviche can be prepped the night before.
- All toppings can be pre-mixed and brought to the grill for easy assembly.
