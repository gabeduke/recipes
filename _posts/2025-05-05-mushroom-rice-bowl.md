# Tomato-Mushroom Rice Bowl with Grilled Asparagus and Tofu

A flexible, umami-packed rice bowl using leftover peeled tomatoes, mushrooms, grilled asparagus, and tofu. Perfect for using up fridge extras while keeping it gluten-free, vegetarian, and dairy-free.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked **basmati rice**
- 1–2 cups **peeled tomatoes** (leftover, whole or crushed)
- 1 cup **mushrooms**, chopped
- 1 block **firm tofu**, cubed
- 1 cup **grilled asparagus**, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced
- 1 tbsp **soy sauce** (or tamari for GF)
- 1 tsp **sesame oil**
- Optional: **chili oil**, **lime**, **fresh herbs** (cilantro or basil)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare tofu and mushrooms**:
    - Heat a skillet over medium-high.
    - Add a splash of oil and sauté the tofu cubes until golden.
    - Add mushrooms and continue cooking until they release moisture and brown.

2. **Add aromatics and tomatoes**:
    - Push tofu and mushrooms to the side of the pan.
    - Add garlic and sauté for 30 seconds.
    - Stir in peeled tomatoes, breaking them up with a spoon.
    - Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Let simmer 5–10 minutes until slightly thickened.

3. **Combine with rice**:
    - Add cooked basmati rice and toss until heated through and coated with sauce.

4. **Serve**:
    - Spoon into bowls.
    - Top with grilled asparagus and optional garnishes like chili oil, lime juice, or herbs.

## Notes

- This recipe came from a conversation where the user had leftover **peeled tomatoes**, **mushrooms**, **grilled asparagus**, and **tofu** on hand while cooking **basmati rice**.
- The idea was to build a one-pan rice bowl that blends tomato richness with mushroom umami and tofu protein.
- You can easily adapt this into a curry-style dish by adding curry powder or coconut milk during the tomato simmer step.
- For texture contrast, top with toasted nuts or seeds (e.g. cashews or pumpkin seeds).
- Leftovers reheat well and can be served with a fried egg on top for extra protein (if not strictly vegan).
