### Spicy Grilled Wings with Tahini-Tomato Glaze
Served with lime-herb quinoa and grilled zucchini

**Prep Time:** 15 min  
**Marinade Time:** 2–4 hours  
**Cook Time:** ~30 min  
**Serves:** 4

---

### Ingredients

**For the Marinade:**

- 3–4 garlic cloves
- 1/2 onion
- 1 jalapeño (seeded or not, to taste)
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 small handful Vietnamese coriander (or cilantro)
- 2 tbsp coconut aminos
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Optional: splash of soy or fish sauce for depth

**For the Glaze:**

- Reserved marinade (see instructions)
- 5–6 cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1–2 tsp honey
- 1 tbsp tahini
- Water as needed to thin

**Wings:**

- 2–3 lbs chicken wings
- Salt (1–1.5 tsp) for seasoning before grilling

**Quinoa:**

- 1 cup quinoa, cooked
- Juice of 1 lime
- Chopped Vietnamese coriander
- Olive oil & salt to taste

**Zucchini:**

- 2 medium zucchini, sliced lengthwise
- Olive oil & salt

---

### Instructions

1. **Marinade & Wings**

   Blend all marinade ingredients into a paste.  
   Toss wings to coat and marinate 2–4 hours.  
   Before grilling, remove wings from marinade, pat dry, and season generously with salt.

2. **Glaze**

   Pour marinade into a saucepan with cherry tomatoes.  
   Simmer 10–15 min, adding water as needed to prevent sticking.  
   Stir in honey and tahini.  
   Simmer until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon.  
   Strain if desired.

3. **Grill**

   Grill wings over medium heat, turning until browned and 165°F inside (20–25 min).  
   Toss in glaze before serving.  
   Grill zucchini slices with olive oil and salt until charred and tender.

4. **Quinoa**

   Toss warm quinoa with lime juice, chopped Vietnamese coriander, a drizzle of olive oil, and salt.

---

### Notes

The glaze can be doubled and saved—great on tofu, roasted carrots, or rice bowls.  
Vietnamese coriander adds a peppery, citrusy brightness. Use cilantro or mint as substitutes if needed.
