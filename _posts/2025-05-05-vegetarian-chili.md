# Vegetarian Chili (Foundational Recipe)

A flexible, hearty chili base perfect for customizing based on what you have on hand. This version is gluten-free, dairy-free, and vegetarian. You can swap in any vegetables or beans you’ve got lying around.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil  
- 1 medium onion, diced  
- 2–3 garlic cloves, minced  
- 1 bell pepper, diced  
- 1 medium carrot, diced  
- 1 zucchini, diced *(optional)*  
- 2 cups cooked beans *(black beans, kidney beans, or a mix)*  
- 1 cup corn kernels *(fresh or frozen)*  
- 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes *(or 2 cups fresh diced tomatoes)*  
- 1 cup vegetable broth *(or water, adjust for desired consistency)*  
- 1 tbsp tomato paste  
- 2 tsp chili powder  
- 1 tsp ground cumin  
- 1 tsp smoked paprika *(optional)*  
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano  
- Salt and pepper to taste  
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper *(optional, for heat)*

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the onion, garlic, bell pepper, and carrot. Sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano. Cook for 1 minute to bloom the spices.
4. Add diced tomatoes, tomato paste, and broth. Stir well.
5. Mix in beans, corn, and zucchini (if using).
6. Bring to a simmer. Let it cook uncovered for 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, and cayenne to taste.
8. Serve hot with toppings like avocado, cilantro, tortilla chips, or dairy-free sour cream.

## Notes
- This recipe was shared as a **base to modify based on what you have on hand**, as requested by the original poster. Common swaps include sweet potatoes, squash, celery, or leftover cooked grains.
- It was designed to be **vegetarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free**, suitable for households with dietary restrictions.
- Great for batch cooking or freezing.
- Can be cooked in a slow cooker after sautéing the aromatics, or made in an Instant Pot on the sauté + pressure cook setting.
