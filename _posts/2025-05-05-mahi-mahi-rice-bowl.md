# Mahi Mahi Rice Bowls with Quick Pickled Onions and Garlicky Leeks

A fresh, flavorful dinner made with frozen mahi mahi, leftover pasta sauce, and pantry-friendly ingredients like rice, garlic, and vinegar. This combo brings together tomato-rich sauce, lightly spiced fish, savory leeks, and zippy pickled onions for a satisfying bowl.

---

## Ingredients

### For the Bowl
- 2 slices mahi mahi, thawed and cubed
- 2 cups cooked rice (any variety)
- ½ cup pasta sauce (leftover or homemade)
- 1–2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp chili powder or smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 tbsp oil (for pan)

### Garlicky Leeks
- 1–2 leeks (white and light green parts), thinly sliced and washed
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter alternative
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Splash of broth, water, or white wine (optional)

### Quick Pickled Red Onions
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
- ½ cup apple cider vinegar (or white vinegar)
- 1 cup hot water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar (optional)
- Optional: pinch chili flakes, peppercorns, or garlic slices

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: Pickle the Onions
1. Place thinly sliced red onions in a bowl or jar.
2. In another bowl, mix vinegar, hot water, salt, and sugar until dissolved.
3. Pour over the onions. Let sit for at least 15 minutes (longer = better flavor). Store in the fridge for up to a week.

### Step 2: Cook the Leeks
1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds, then add leeks.
3. Season with salt and pepper, and sauté until softened and golden, 8–10 minutes.
4. Add a splash of broth or water if needed. Optional: finish with lemon juice or a pinch of thyme.

### Step 3: Cook the Mahi Mahi
**Option 1 (Recommended)**:  
1. Season cubed mahi with cumin, chili powder, salt, and garlic.
2. Heat a separate pan with oil and sear mahi for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.

**Option 2 (One-Pan)**:  
1. After cooking the leeks, push them to one side of the pan.
2. Add a little more oil, then add the seasoned mahi and cook until done, tossing together at the end.

### Step 4: Assemble the Bowls
1. Layer cooked rice into bowls.
2. Spoon over warm pasta sauce.
3. Add leeks, seared mahi mahi, and pickled onions.
4. Finish with a squeeze of lime, fresh herbs (optional), or hot sauce if desired.

---

## Notes

- This meal came together from a user who had leftover pasta sauce and frozen mahi mahi and wanted to combine them into a rice-based dinner.
- The fish is seasoned with cumin and garlic to complement the tomato base without overpowering it.
- Leeks add sweetness and depth and can be cooked separately or with the fish.
- Quick pickled red onions bring a bright, acidic contrast to balance the dish and are easy to prep ahead.
- Great with avocado, cabbage slaw, or cilantro if you want to bulk it up even more.
- Cooking the fish separately gives a crispier result, while the one-pan option is faster and saves dishes.
