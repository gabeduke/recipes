# Garlic Butter Chicken Thighs (Quick Post-Soccer Meal)

A fast, protein-packed way to cook boneless skinless chicken thighs after a long day—or in this case, after a soccer match. This dish comes together in under 15 minutes and hits the spot when you're tired and hungry.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter (or dairy-free substitute)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced *(or 1/2 tsp garlic powder if you're tired)*
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika *(optional but great)*
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano or thyme *(optional)*
- 1 tbsp lemon juice *(or splash of vinegar)*

## Instructions
1. **Heat a skillet** over medium-high heat and add the olive oil.
2. **Pat the chicken dry** and season both sides with salt, pepper, paprika, and herbs.
3. **Sear the chicken** thighs for 4–5 minutes on each side until nicely browned and cooked through.
4. **Add garlic and butter**, stirring for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. **Deglaze with lemon juice** and cook for another 30 seconds.
6. **Remove from heat** and let rest for 2 minutes before serving.

## Notes
- This recipe was written for a quick, satisfying meal after a late-night soccer match when you're too tired for anything elaborate.
- Boneless, skinless chicken thighs cook quickly and stay juicy, making them ideal for this situation.
- Serve with whatever you have—rice, greens, or even straight from the pan.
- If you’re avoiding dairy, use a plant-based butter or just olive oil.

## Variations
- Swap thighs for chicken breast cutlets (reduce cook time).
- Add a pinch of chili flakes for heat.
- Finish with chopped parsley or chives for freshness.
