# Lime Marinated Grilled Chicken Thighs

A citrusy, smoky marinade that pairs beautifully with chicken thighs for the grill. The lime brightens the meat while spices and honey add depth and balance.

## Ingredients
- Juice of 2 limes
- Zest of 1 lime
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp chili powder or chipotle powder *(optional, for smoky heat)*
- 1 tbsp honey or agave syrup
- Salt & pepper to taste
- ¼ cup chopped cilantro *(optional)*
- 2 lbs chicken thighs (bone-in or boneless)

## Instructions
1. In a bowl or zip-top bag, combine all marinade ingredients and stir well.
2. Add chicken thighs and toss to coat thoroughly.
3. Marinate in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes, preferably 2–4 hours.
4. Preheat grill to medium-high heat.
5. Grill the chicken thighs until charred and cooked through, turning as needed, about 6–8 minutes per side.
6. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- **Optional additions**: Add sliced jalapeños or a splash of orange juice for more complexity. Oregano or thyme can add a herbal twist.
- **Serving suggestions**: Serve with grilled vegetables, cilantro-lime rice, or a cabbage slaw for a fresh, balanced meal.
- **Make it a meal**: This marinade also works well on tofu or portobello mushrooms for a vegetarian option.
