# Mushroom Risotto with Overwintered Kale

A cozy, savory risotto that pairs beautifully with early spring kale harvested from overwintered plants. This recipe includes multiple ways to incorporate kale—stirred in, blended, or served on the side—so you can use what’s freshest and works best for your meal.

## Ingredients

### For the Risotto
- 1 cup Arborio rice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter (or dairy-free alternative)
- 1 small onion or 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lb mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, or mixed), sliced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine (optional)
- 4 cups warm vegetable broth
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: 2 tbsp nutritional yeast or dairy-free cheese for creaminess

### For the Kale (choose one or more methods)
- 2–3 cups kale leaves, stems removed and chopped

**Stir-In Option:**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper

**Green Purée Option:**
- Blanched kale
- 1/4 cup vegetable broth
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

**Sautéed Side Option:**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- Chili flakes (optional)
- Splash of lemon juice or vinegar

## Instructions

### Make the Risotto
1. Warm the broth and keep it on low heat.
2. In a large pan, heat olive oil and butter. Add onion and cook until soft.
3. Add garlic and mushrooms. Sauté until mushrooms are browned and liquid has mostly evaporated.
4. Stir in Arborio rice and toast for 1–2 minutes.
5. Add white wine and let it absorb.
6. Begin adding broth 1 ladle at a time, stirring frequently. Let each ladle absorb before adding the next, about 20 minutes total.
7. Season with salt, pepper, and optional nutritional yeast.

### Kale Options

#### 1. Stir-In Kale
- Sauté chopped kale in olive oil for 3–5 minutes. Stir into risotto during the last 5 minutes of cooking.

#### 2. Green Kale Purée
- Blanch kale in boiling water for 1 minute, then shock in ice water. Blend with broth and lemon juice. Stir into risotto after rice is cooked, before serving.

#### 3. Garlicky Sautéed Kale (Side)
- Heat olive oil in a skillet. Add garlic and chili flakes.
- Add chopped kale, season, and sauté until wilted but vibrant.
- Finish with a splash of lemon juice or vinegar.

## Notes
- This recipe was inspired by a Virginia spring harvest: overwintered kale was thriving, producing sweet and tender leaves, even as some plants began to bolt.
- Stirring kale directly into the risotto creates a heartier, one-dish meal. Puréeing the kale makes for a visually striking dish with a smoother texture.
- Serving kale on the side lets it shine with bold garlicky or acidic flavors to contrast the rich risotto.
- Ideal for seasonal cooking—use whatever variety of kale you have (curly, lacinato, Russian).
- To make it fully dairy-free, replace butter with olive oil and use nutritional yeast instead of cheese.

## Optional Additions
- Top with crispy roasted mushrooms, poached egg, or kale chips.
- Add peas or asparagus tips for a more spring-forward version.
