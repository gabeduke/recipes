# Basic Lentils Recipe with Variations

A foundational lentil recipe with tips and several directions you can take it—perfect for batch cooking, weeknight meals, or spicing up your pantry staples.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dry lentils (brown or green recommended)
- 3 cups water or vegetable broth
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Rinse lentils in a fine mesh strainer under cool water.
2. In a medium pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add diced onion and sauté until soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add lentils, water or broth, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil.
5. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook uncovered for 30–35 minutes, or until lentils are tender.
6. Add salt and pepper in the last 10 minutes of cooking. Adjust seasoning to taste.
7. Discard bay leaf before serving.

## Notes

- Use a **2:1 water ratio** for firmer lentils or a **3:1 ratio** for soupier results.
- Don’t salt at the beginning—it can make the lentils tough.
- Store cooked lentils in the fridge for up to 5 days or freeze in batches.

## Variations

### Indian-Style Dal
- Substitute red lentils.
- Add: 1 tsp cumin seeds, ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp garam masala.
- Optional: 1 chopped tomato and 1 tsp grated ginger.
- Finish with a tarka (tempered ghee or oil with mustard seeds, garlic, and chili flakes).

### Mediterranean
- Add: 1 chopped carrot and 1 celery stalk with the onions.
- Finish with lemon juice, chopped parsley, and a drizzle of good olive oil.

### Latin-Inspired
- Add: 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp ground cumin, and 1 chopped tomato.
- Serve with rice, a squeeze of lime, and chopped cilantro.

### Hearty Stew
- Add: 1 chopped potato or sweet potato and a handful of chopped kale/spinach during last 10 minutes.
- Finish with: 1 tbsp red wine vinegar or sherry vinegar.

## Serving Ideas
- Spoon over rice or quinoa.
- Use as a filling for wraps or tacos.
- Stir into soups or grain bowls.
- Serve alongside roasted vegetables or eggs.

## Storage
- **Fridge:** Up to 5 days
- **Freezer:** Up to 3 months
