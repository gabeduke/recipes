# Gazpacho

A refreshing chilled soup made from ripe tomatoes, cucumbers, and peppers—perfect for hot days. This version is naturally gluten-free, dairy-free, and vegetarian.

## Ingredients
- 6 ripe tomatoes, cored and roughly chopped
- 1 cucumber, peeled, seeded, and chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, cored and chopped
- 1 small red onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 cups tomato juice
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for spice)
- Fresh basil or parsley, for garnish

## Instructions
1. **Blend the Base**  
   Combine tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, red onion, garlic, and tomato juice in a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth or leave slightly chunky for texture.
2. **Season**  
   Add olive oil, red wine vinegar, salt, black pepper, and cayenne (if using). Blend briefly to incorporate.
3. **Chill**  
   Transfer to a large bowl or container. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours so the flavors meld.
4. **Serve**  
   Stir well and ladle into bowls. Garnish with fresh basil or parsley. Optional: drizzle with a little more olive oil before serving.

## Notes
- This recipe was requested as a quick and simple gazpacho for hot days, and formatted in markdown for easy inclusion in a recipe site.
- It's a perfect make-ahead summer dish and suits gluten-free, vegetarian, and dairy-free diets.
- You can strain the soup through a fine mesh sieve after blending for a smoother texture.
- Serve alongside crusty gluten-free bread or keep it light with just a garnish of herbs.
